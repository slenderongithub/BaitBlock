Letters To The Editor
Is this a fact-checker?

No. BaitBlock judges how a story is framed and sourced, not whether its facts are true. A well-written article can still be wrong, and a clumsy one can be right. Use the verdict as a reason to read carefully, then verify the claims it lists.

How accurate is the headline check?

The headline-style model is a logistic regression trained on the public "Stop Clickbait" corpus (32,000 labelled headlines). On 6,400 held-out headlines it scores 98% accuracy. It is combined with explainable rules, so every point of the score traces back to a specific signal you can read.

Why is one red flag enough for a high score?

Dimensions combine like independent alarms: one strong signal (a fake outlet domain, a miracle-cure pitch) raises the score on its own, several moderate ones compound, and faint noise across many checks is ignored.

What does "analysis confidence" mean?

How much text we had to work with. A missing headline, a very short body, a non-English page or a summary-only RSS copy all lower it. Treat low-confidence results as rough.

Do you store the links I check?

No accounts, no cookies, no database. Fetched pages are cached in memory for a few minutes so a retry is fast, then discarded.
